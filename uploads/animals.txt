Dinosaur Knowledge Sheet Overview Dinosaurs were a diverse group of reptiles that lived during the Mesozoic Era. They first appeared approximately 230 million years ago during the Late Triassic Period and dominated terrestrial ecosystems until the end of the Cretaceous Period about 66 million years ago. The Mesozoic Era is divided into three periods: Triassic, Jurassic, and Cretaceous. Tyrannosaurus rex lived during the Late Cretaceous Period approximately 68–66 million years ago. Triceratops lived during the Late Cretaceous Period. Triceratops and T. rex lived during roughly the same geological time period. Stegosaurus lived during the Late Jurassic Period approximately 155–150 million years ago. Stegosaurus lived millions of years before T. rex. Velociraptor lived approximately 75–71 million years ago.
