# All About Elephants

## What is an elephant?

Elephants are the largest land animals on Earth. They live in different parts of Africa and Asia and usually live in groups called **herds**.

## Why does an elephant have a long trunk?

An elephant's trunk is like a very useful combination of a **nose and a hand**.

Elephants use their trunks to:

* Smell food, water, and other elephants.
* Pick up food such as grass, leaves, and fruit.
* Drink water by sucking it into their trunks and then spraying it into their mouths.
* Touch and communicate with other elephants.
* Breathe and make sounds.

An elephant's trunk has thousands of muscles, which allow it to make very small and careful movements as well as powerful ones.

## ⭐ Amazing Fact

Elephants can use their trunks to pick up objects that are very small, such as a single blade of grass.
