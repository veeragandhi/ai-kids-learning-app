Plants need several things to grow well. They need water, sunlight, air, and nutrients from the soil.

Sunlight gives plants energy to make their own food. Water travels from the roots to the rest of the plant. The air provides carbon dioxide, which plants use when making food. The roots also take nutrients and water from the soil.

A seed contains a tiny baby plant and stored food. When a seed gets enough water, the seed coat becomes soft and the seed begins to germinate.

First, a small root grows downward into the soil. Then a shoot grows upward toward the light. Eventually, leaves open and begin making food for the growing plant.
